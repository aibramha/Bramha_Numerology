Privacy Policy for Bramha Numerology

Last Updated: January 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bramha Numerology ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Personal Details: Name, birth date, birth time (optional), and location information you enter for numerology calculations
  • Profile Data: User profiles you create, including relationship types (Self, Spouse, Child, Parent, Friend, etc.)
  • Voice Input: Audio recordings when you use voice-to-text features (processed locally, not stored)
  • Camera Access: Images when using signature analysis or document scanning (processed locally, not stored)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Usage Data: Features used, calculation history stored locally
  • Advertising ID: For serving personalized ads (can be reset in device settings)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for:

  • Performing numerology calculations and providing personalized insights
  • Storing your profiles locally on your device for convenience
  • Sending scheduled notifications for daily insights (only if you enable them)
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Displaying relevant advertisements through Google AdMob

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your personal data is stored ONLY on your device. We use:

  • SQLite database for profiles and calculation history
  • SharedPreferences for app settings
  • Local file storage for exported PDFs

4.2 No Cloud Storage

We do NOT upload, sync, or store your personal information on any servers. Your numerology data never leaves your device.

4.3 On-Device Processing

All AI/ML features (TensorFlow Lite, ML Kit) run entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers for processing.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Usage data

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Opt-out of personalized ads: Google Ads Settings

5.2 No Analytics or Tracking

We do NOT use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or any tracking services. We respect your privacy completely.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Data Access and Deletion

  • View Your Data: All data is visible in Profile Manager within the app
  • Delete Profiles: Tap delete button in Profile Manager
  • Clear All Data: Go to app settings and clear app data, or uninstall the app
  • Export Data: Use the export feature to save your data as PDF

7.2 Advertising Choices

  • Reset your Advertising ID in device settings
  • Limit ad tracking in your device privacy settings
  • Ads are required for the free version of the app

7.3 Permission Management

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's app settings. The app will continue to work with reduced functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents can create profiles for their children and manage all data through the Profile Manager.

9. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you:

  • Delete specific profiles or data
  • Clear the app's data
  • Uninstall the application

We do not have access to your data and cannot delete it remotely.

10. International Users

Since all data is stored locally on your device, no data transfer across borders occurs. The app works the same way regardless of your location.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Showing an in-app notification
  • Posting the new policy in the app's "About" section

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us:

Email: privacy@bramhanumerology.com

App: Use "Contact Support" in app settings

Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours

13. Legal Compliance

This app complies with:

  • Google Play Store Developer Policies
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

14. Data Security Measures

  • All data encrypted using Android's built-in encryption
  • No network transmission of personal data
  • Local-only AI/ML processing
  • No third-party data sharing (except ads)
  • Regular security updates via Play Store
